MediSupply Hub Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Effective date: November 3, 2026

MediSupply Hub (“MediSupply Hub,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and handled when you use our mobile application or website.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect information such as:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and account details
  • State, delivery address, and other information you provide in your profile
  • Profile picture, if you choose to upload one
  • Account identifiers used to create, authenticate, and manage your account
  • Order details, items purchased, delivery information, and purchase history
  • Device, app, and technical information needed to operate, maintain, and secure the service

Please provide only information that is necessary for your use of the service.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Provide app functionality
  • Process and fulfil orders
  • Coordinate deliveries
  • Send transactional notifications
  • Respond to support requests
  • Improve our services
  • Prevent fraud and security incidents
  • Meet applicable legal obligations

3. Notifications and Messages

MediSupply Hub may send account or order-related notifications, such as order updates and delivery notices. We may also send service announcements or promotional messages where permitted. You can manage device notification permissions through your device settings.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose relevant information to service providers who help us operate the app, such as hosting and database providers, payment processors, and delivery or logistics partners. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or our services.

We aim to limit information shared with service providers to what is reasonably needed for their services.

5. Payments

Payments may be handled by third-party payment providers. Payment providers process payment information under their own terms and privacy policies. MediSupply Hub does not intend to store full payment card details directly.

6. Storage and Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. Our services may rely on third-party infrastructure, including Firebase or other hosting and technology providers. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain necessary business records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce applicable agreements. When information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to delete or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable law.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your MediSupply Hub account and associated personal data by visiting our Account Deletion page or emailing Medisupplyhub@gmail.com.

We may ask for information needed to verify that you own the account. Some records may be retained where required by law or necessary to resolve outstanding transactions, disputes, or security issues. We will explain if any information cannot be deleted and why, where applicable.

9. Children’s Privacy

MediSupply Hub is not intended for children where use or collection of their information is prohibited by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided us information improperly, contact us so we can review the matter.

10. Third-Party Services

Features of MediSupply Hub may depend on third-party services. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Please review their policies when applicable.

11. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or ask questions about how it is handled. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or practices change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the effective date.
